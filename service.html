Oil & Gas Projects

Pipeline Works

Our expertise in pipeline design, construction, and maintenance ensures the integrity of crude oil, natural gas, and refined product transportation networks. We implement advanced welding techniques, corrosion-resistant materials, and real-time monitoring systems to enhance pipeline durability and prevent leaks.

Our pipeline integrity programs include hydrostatic testing, inline inspections (ILI), and cathodic protection to safeguard assets and ensure compliance with industry safety standards.

Hydrostatic testing & inline inspections (ILI)

Cathodic protection & corrosion-resistant materials

Tank Works

We design, construct, and maintain aboveground and underground storage tanks for crude oil, refined products, and petrochemicals. Our tank services include API 650 and API 653 compliance, leak detection systems, secondary containment solutions, and floating roof technology to minimize vapor emissions.

We also conduct structural integrity assessments, corrosion control, and periodic inspections to enhance tank safety and longevity.

At GFN, our commitment to innovation, safety, and sustainability ensures that every aspect of our oil & gas infrastructure meets the highest industry standards, helping our clients maintain secure and efficient operations in an ever-evolving energy landscape.

API 650 & API 653 compliance

Floating roof technology for vapor emission control

Secondary containment & leak detection systems

Pumping Stations

We design, construct, and maintain oil and gas pumping stations that support the safe and efficient transfer of crude oil, refined products, and other hydrocarbons throughout pipeline and processing networks.

Our pumping station services include pump and piping system installation, flow and pressure control, metering systems, filtration, surge protection, automated monitoring, and emergency shutdown systems. We also provide equipment inspections, preventative maintenance, corrosion control, and system upgrades to improve reliability, efficiency, and operational safety.

We have successfully completed several pumping station projects.

Pump & piping system installation

Flow & pressure control, surge protection

Automated monitoring & emergency shutdown systems

Refinery Maintenance Works

GFN supports refinery development, modernization, and maintenance with a focus on efficiency, emissions reduction, and process optimization. Our engineering and maintenance teams ensure that refinery operations run at peak efficiency while adhering to strict environmental and regulatory standards.
