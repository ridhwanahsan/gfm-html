Who We Are

From IETC to GFN Projects Inc.

GFN initially started as IETC, a multi-national company, which has a background of experience in dozens of infrastructure projects. Projects completed successfully were in the oil & gas services, pipelines, storage tanks, pumping stations and refinery maintenance.

GFN represents a new vision and area of operations, with the aim of implementing new leading technologies and opportunities for growth.

GFN offers a range of complementary services to the industry, such as consulting and evaluation of the feasibility of the project, plant optimization, upgrading, retrofit, and maintenance programs for upcoming projects.

Pipeline Infrastructure

Design, construction & maintenance of oil and gas pipelines.

Storage & Processing

API-compliant tanks, pumping stations & refinery maintenance.
